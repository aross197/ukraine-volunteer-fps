# Master Design Document — Ukraine Volunteer FPS

## 1. Vision

A PC-first, Call of Duty-style modern military FPS set in Ukraine from the first day of the full-scale invasion (24 February 2022) through November 2026.

The player is always an **international volunteer** from their own country who has joined Ukrainian forces or the International Legion. The player **never** controls or sides with Russian / aggressor forces.

The game prioritizes:
- Real weapons and vehicle designations
- Real Ukrainian units and period-correct appearance
- Strict historical timeline of equipment availability
- Authentic tactical evolution of the conflict

While keeping COD-style arcade movement, gunfeel, and pacing.

## 2. Platform

- Primary: PC (Windows)
- Future: Possible console ports (including PS5) after PC version is solid

## 3. Core Systems

### 3.1 Vehicle Capture & Tractor Recovery
- Players can secure abandoned or lightly defended enemy vehicles.
- In 2022 content, civilian tractors are usable tools for towing immobilized / abandoned armor (historically accurate).
- Captured vehicles are driveable but come with drawbacks (reliability, ammo types, markings, handling).

### 3.2 Weapon Progression
- Classic level-based unlock system.
- All unlocks are **timeline-locked**. A weapon or system only becomes available when it historically appeared in meaningful numbers.

### 3.3 Units & Appearance
- Real Ukrainian unit names and structures.
- Period-correct uniforms, camouflage (MM-14, multicam variants, etc.), patches, plate carriers, helmets, and loadouts.
- International volunteers show national flavor through patches and minor kit differences while remaining part of Ukrainian / Legion units.
- Enemy forces use correct Russian designations and period-accurate gear.

### 3.4 Multiplayer
- Players only ever appear as international volunteers on the Ukrainian side.
- Period-locked playlists keep loadouts and vehicles historically accurate.

## 4. Timeline Structure

- **2022 Early (Feb–May)**: Invasion chaos, Kyiv axis, Hostomel, high volume of abandoned Russian equipment, tractor recovery common.
- **2022 Mid–Late**: Arrival of HIMARS, M777, first major counteroffensives (Kharkiv, Kherson).
- **2023**: Bradleys, Leopards, Bakhmut, summer counteroffensive.
- **2024–2026**: F-16s, dense FPV / fiber-optic drone warfare, layered Western air defense, long-range strike systems, evolved tactics.
