# Equipment Timeline (Real Systems Only)

This document will list real weapons, vehicles, and systems available in each major period of the war.

## Structure

### February – May 2022
- Ukrainian / Volunteer starting loadouts
- Common Russian vehicles that can be captured
- Early ATGMs (Javelin, NLAW, Stugna-P, etc.)

### June – December 2022
- Arrival of HIMARS, M777, additional Western systems

### 2023
- Bradleys, Leopard 2, Challenger 2, expanded artillery and air defense

### 2024 – 2026
- F-16, Mirage 2000-5, dense FPV and fiber-optic drones, long-range strike systems, modern layered air defense

---

**Rule**: Nothing is available before it historically appeared in meaningful numbers.
